Summary

This policy applies to the Android app 1000 Days. It works without an account. Your habits and your progress are stored on your device. I do not run a server for the app and have no access to your data.

There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

Your data leaves the device in two ways only: when you export a backup as a file yourself, and through Android’s backup to your Google account if it is switched on on your device. In addition, the app asks Google Play whether a new version is available.

Which data, what for, on which legal basis

4.1 Habits and progress

Data
everything you enter: name of the habit, goals, trigger, icon, color and reminder times. Plus what results from it: the days on which you completed it, streaks, level, points and the name of your universe.
Purpose
keeping your habits and showing your progress
Legal basis
You process the data on your own device. It is not transmitted to me. For storage on the device see section 5.
Location
the app’s own storage on your device
Retention
until you remove a habit, delete your data or uninstall the app

Habits can reveal something about your health or your everyday life. You decide what you enter.

4.2 Settings

Language, appearance, accessibility options, sound and vibration, and which tips you have already seen. This, too, is stored on your device only.

4.3 Reminders

The app schedules reminders itself on your device. There is no service that sends them from outside. A reminder shows the name of the habit, also on the lock screen. With the buttons of the notification you can check the habit off or be reminded later.

4.4 Widget

The widget on the home screen shows streak, progress and open habits. The information for it stays on the device.

4.5 Backups, export and import

Data
all data from 4.1 and 4.2
Purpose
bringing back an earlier state or moving to another device
Location
The Time Machine keeps backups in the app’s own storage: by hand, daily and before deleting. An export writes a file to your documents or downloads. That file is outside the app and is not encrypted.
Retention
Time Machine backups until you delete them. An exported file until you remove it yourself; “Delete Data” in the app does not reach it.

4.6 Updates and rating through Google Play

How it works
When opened, the app asks Google Play whether a new version is available. After some days of use, the Google Play rating dialog may appear once. For this the app counts on the device on how many days you have used it.
Data
The app itself sends no content. Google Play processes information about device, app version and Google account under Google’s rules.
Purpose
keeping the app up to date and secure, enabling feedback
Legal basis
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: an up-to-date, secure app.

4.7 Links from the app

The links to juek3y.com and to Buy Me a Coffee only open when you tap them, and then in your browser. The policies of the respective site apply there.

4.8 Contact by email

Data
your email address and the content of your message
Purpose
answering your inquiry
Legal basis
Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, otherwise (f) (legitimate interest: answering inquiries)
Retention
I delete the message once the inquiry is settled, unless a statutory retention period applies.

Storing and accessing information on your device (§ 25 TDDDG)

The app stores on your device what it needs to work: your habits and settings, the Time Machine backups, the information for the widget, the scheduled reminders and the counter for the rating dialog. This is strictly necessary for the app to provide the functions you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

The app sets no cookies and no identifiers for advertising or tracking. Nobody reads this information from outside.

Permissions of the app

Notifications
for reminders. The app only asks when you create a reminder. Without it there are no reminders; everything else works.
Exact alarms
so that reminders arrive on time. Without it they arrive at roughly the chosen time.
Start after reboot
so that reminders and widget are correct again after the device restarts
Vibration
for haptic feedback. Can be switched off in the settings.
Internet
only for updates and rating through Google Play (4.6). The app itself contacts no servers.
Storage
only up to Android 9, to save an exported backup

Recipients and third-party services

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting services. All images, sounds and fonts are part of the app.

7.1 Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. The provider for users in the European Economic Area is Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google Play delivers the app and its updates and shows the rating dialog (4.6). Android and Google Play may also collect technical data such as crash reports and installation figures, depending on the settings of your device. I have no influence on this. Privacy information: policies.google.com

7.2 Android backup

If Android’s backup is switched on on your device, Android can back up the app’s data to your Google account and restore it on a new device. You control this in the Android settings under “Backup”. The recipient is then Google, not me.

7.3 Buy Me a Coffee

The link in the profile leads to buymeacoffee.com. Data only flows when you tap it and open the site in your browser.

Servers of JueK3y

I do not run a server that receives data from the app.

Visiting this page: How the website juek3y.com handles access data is described in the website privacy policy. This page sets no cookies and loads no third-party content.

Security

Your data is in the app’s own storage. Android separates it from other apps. The app does not encrypt the data additionally: it is as secure as your device, so ideally with a screen lock.

An exported backup is a readable file. Keep it where only you have access.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

Since I do not store any data about you, I can generally not provide information about stored data. You manage your data yourself in the app:

view and change
directly at the habit
take with you
Profile → Data Vault → “Save Now” → “Also export as file”
delete
Profile → “Delete Data” → “Delete everything permanently” removes all data and backups in the app. Uninstalling deletes them as well. You delete exported files and an Android backup separately.

This app does not base any processing on consent. You can withdraw permissions at any time in the Android settings. If you have written to me, you can exercise your rights regarding that message with me: hello@juek3y.com

Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example where you live. The authority responsible for me is:

No automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making or profiling under Art. 22 GDPR.
