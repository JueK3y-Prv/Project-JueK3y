Summary

This policy applies to the website juek3y.com. It works without cookies, without tracking and without analytics. Fonts, images and code are served from juek3y.com itself; nothing is loaded from other servers.

When you open the site, the technical service providers that deliver it (Cloudflare and Netlify) process your IP address and technical access data.

If you write to me through the contact form, Netlify stores your message and sends it to me by email. Something is stored in your browser only if you change the language or the color scheme yourself.

Controller

Hauptstr. 32D
86922 Eresing
Germany
Email: hello@juek3y.com

Full details are in the legal notice.

Which data, what for, on which legal basis

4.1 Opening the website

To show a page, your browser has to request it. This necessarily produces technical access data.

Data
IP address, date and time, requested address, amount of data transferred, status code, browser and operating system, previously visited page (if your browser sends it)
Purpose
delivering the website, detecting and fending off faults and attacks
Legal basis
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: secure and stable operation.
Location
servers of Cloudflare and Netlify (section 7)
Retention
I keep no logs myself. According to the providers, access logs are kept only briefly, by Netlify for less than 30 days.

4.2 Contact form

At the end of the home page you can write to me. The form is only transmitted when you press “Send message”.

Data
your email address, your name (optional), your message, the language of the page, date and time, as well as IP address and browser identifier
Purpose
reading and answering your message, fending off spam
Legal basis
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: answering inquiries and protecting the form against abuse. If it concerns a contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR applies.
Location
Netlify receives the message, stores it and sends it to me by email (section 7).
Retention
until your request is settled. After that I delete the message at Netlify and in my mailbox unless a statutory retention period applies.

Against spam the form contains an invisible field that only programs fill in. In addition, Netlify automatically checks every submission for spam. There is no captcha.

4.3 Contact by email

Data
your email address and the content of your message
Purpose
answering your inquiry
Legal basis
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: answering inquiries. If it concerns a contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR applies.
Location
my email mailbox (section 7)
Retention
until your request is settled. After that I delete the message unless a statutory retention period applies.

4.4 Language and color scheme

On your first visit the website follows the language and the color scheme of your device. If you change one of them yourself, your browser remembers the choice (section 5). This information does not leave your device.

4.5 My apps

The apps have their own policies: TravelFlow, Saldo and 1000 Days.

Storing and accessing information on your device (§ 25 TDDDG)

The website sets no cookies.

It puts at most two entries into your browser’s local storage, and only if you change the respective setting yourself:

juek3y.lang
the chosen language (de or en)
juek3y.theme
the chosen color scheme (light or dark)

Both are strictly necessary to keep the setting you explicitly asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). No consent is needed for this. The entries stay until you delete the site data in your browser.

Permissions

The website requests no permissions from your device: no location, no camera, no microphone, no notifications.

Recipients and third-party services

The website contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services, no embedded content from other providers and no fonts from other servers. The only recipients of your data are the following technical service providers.

7.1 Cloudflare

All requests to juek3y.com pass through the network of Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Cloudflare receives the request, fends off attacks and passes it on to Netlify. In doing so Cloudflare processes the access data from section 4.1 on my behalf (Art. 28 GDPR), also in the USA. This is based on Cloudflare’s data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses and on Cloudflare’s certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Privacy information: cloudflare.com

7.2 Netlify

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (101 2nd Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA). Netlify delivers the pages and processes the access data from section 4.1. Netlify also receives the messages from the contact form (section 4.2), stores them and checks them for spam with the Akismet service provided by Automattic. Netlify works on my behalf (Art. 28 GDPR), also in the USA. This is based on Netlify’s data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses and on Netlify’s certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Privacy information: netlify.com

7.3 Email

Messages to hello@juek3y.com are received by the forwarding service ImprovMX and passed on to my mailbox at Gmail. Gmail is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). According to ImprovMX, delivery logs are kept for 7 days. Privacy information: improvmx.com, policies.google.com

Servers of JueK3y

I do not run a server of my own for the website. It is hosted by Netlify and delivered through Cloudflare (section 7). The small relay services for the TravelFlow app are described in its privacy policy.

Security

The website can only be reached encrypted via HTTPS, and so can the contact form. Because it loads nothing from other servers, nobody except the providers named above learns of your visit.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

If you have written to me, you can exercise your rights regarding that message with me: hello@juek3y.com. You delete language and color scheme yourself by removing the site data in your browser.

This website does not base any processing on consent. That is why there is no consent prompt here.

Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example where you live. The authority responsible for me is:

No automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making or profiling under Art. 22 GDPR.
