Summary

Saldo is a personal finance app that runs on your own server. Your data stays with you: JueK3y does not operate a service that financial data is sent to, and there is no user account with JueK3y.

There is no telemetry, no advertising, no analytics and no AI services that receive financial data.

Only what you set up goes out: bank sync through Enable Banking, price lookups with the identifier of a security, and notifications to an address of your choice.

Saldo is currently operated privately and non-commercially: by the provider for his own household.

Which data, what for, on which legal basis

The sole purpose is an overview of your own finances. Where Saldo is used exclusively for personal or household purposes, the GDPR does not apply to this processing (Art. 2 (2) (c) GDPR).

4.1 Account information via bank sync

When you connect a bank account, Saldo retrieves data through the regulated account information service Enable Banking and stores it in your installation. Access is read-only. Saldo cannot initiate payments. Saldo never sees or stores your online banking credentials: you sign in with your bank only.

Data
account name, IBAN and type of account; balances; booked transactions with date, amount, currency, name and IBAN of the counterparty and reference
Purpose
seeing accounts and transactions in Saldo without exporting by hand
Legal basis
your explicit consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you give at your bank with strong customer authentication
Location
your installation
Retention
Consent to bank sync is valid for at most 180 days and must then be renewed at the bank. Stored data stays until you delete it.

With joint accounts, the transactions of the co-holder are visible as well. Only connect such an account with their agreement.

4.2 Imported files and your own entries

Data
transactions from files you import yourself, and your entries in the app (plan, pots, assets, notes)
Purpose
the overview of your finances
Location
your installation. The imported file itself is not kept.
Retention
until you delete the data

4.3 Sign-in

You sign in with a passkey. Its public key is stored, along with a session identifier in a technically necessary cookie (section 5).

4.4 Prices for securities

To value your securities, your installation looks up prices. Only the securities identifier (ISIN) or the ticker symbol is transmitted, never quantity or value (section 7). The lookup can be switched off in Saldo.

4.5 Notifications

Notifications exist only if you set them up yourself. They go to your own Home Assistant server or to an address of your choice and contain the title and text of the notice, without amounts by default.

Storing and accessing information on your device (§ 25 TDDDG)

Saldo sets exactly one cookie: the session identifier after sign-in. It is technically necessary to keep you signed in (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). There are no other cookies and no tracking.

Permissions of the app

Saldo runs in the browser and requests no permissions from your device. For sign-in your device uses its passkey.

Recipients and third-party services

Saldo passes no data on to third parties. The only parties involved are:

7.1 Enable Banking

Enable Banking Oy (Finland) is an account information service supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority. It transmits the account information from your bank to your installation and sees the accounts, balances and transactions of the connected accounts in the process. Its privacy policy applies.

7.2 Price sources

Prices come from justETF, with Yahoo Finance as a fallback. If neither source knows a stock in euros, Saldo asks OpenFIGI once for its symbol on a German exchange. Only the ISIN or the ticker symbol is transmitted. For technical reasons the IP address of your server becomes visible.

7.3 Notifications

If you set up notifications: your own Home Assistant server or the address you enter.

7.4 Tailscale

Your devices reach the installation through the private network of Tailscale. The traffic is encrypted. Tailscale sees connection data but no content.

Servers of JueK3y

JueK3y does not operate a server that receives data from Saldo. Your installation runs on your own server.

Visiting this page: How the website juek3y.com handles access data is described in the website privacy policy. This page sets no cookies and loads no third-party content.

Security

  • The database and all backups are encrypted (AES-256).
  • The installation cannot be reached from the internet, only from your private network.
  • The access key for bank sync is stored encrypted in the installation and never in the program code.
  • Backups are kept on a staggered schedule (twelve months at most) and then deleted automatically.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. In Saldo you exercise them yourself:

take with you
System → Export all data
delete
System → Delete all financial data removes all transactions, balances and settings.
withdraw
You can end bank sync at any time under System → Bank sync → Disconnect or at your bank.

Questions about data protection: hello@juek3y.com
