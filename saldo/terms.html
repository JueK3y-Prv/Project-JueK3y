What Saldo is

Saldo is software for keeping an overview of one's own finances. The user runs it on a server of their own. JueK3y does not provide an online service and does not hold users' data.

Saldo is currently not offered to the public. Use is reserved for the provider and their household, privately and free of charge.

Bank sync

  • Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a regulated account information service provider. Its terms apply in addition.
  • Saldo receives read-only access to balances and transactions. Saldo cannot initiate payments.
  • You give consent at your bank. It is valid for at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time in Saldo or at the bank.
  • Only connect accounts you are entitled to use – joint accounts only with the co-holders' agreement.

No financial, tax or legal advice

Saldo shows evaluations, forecasts and hints based on the data available. This is not advice. Forecasts rest on assumptions and may differ from what actually happens. You make decisions on your own responsibility.

Accuracy and availability

Automatic classification (categories, contracts, transfers) and retrieved prices may be wrong or incomplete. Your bank's records are authoritative. There is no entitlement to uninterrupted availability of Saldo or the connected services.

User's responsibility

  • Keep the master key of your installation safe. Without it, backups cannot be restored.
