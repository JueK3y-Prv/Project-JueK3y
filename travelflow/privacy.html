Summary

This policy applies to the TravelFlow Android app. It works without an account. Your travel plans stay on your device, where they are encrypted by default. I have no access to them.

Some features load content from the internet: map, place search, place photos, exchange rates, flight data and travel alerts. The services named below receive your IP address and only what the feature needs, for example a search term or a flight number.

There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

Controller

Julian Kennedy
Hauptstr. 32D
86922 Eresing
Germany
Email: hello@juek3y.com

Full details are in the legal notice. No data protection officer has been appointed, and none is required by law.

What data, for what purpose, on what legal basis

You are not obliged to provide any data. Without an internet connection, the map, place search, photos, current rates, flight data and travel alerts are unavailable. Trip planning itself works offline.

Whatever is stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all app data. Files you saved or shared yourself (backup, calendar file, budget image) and trip calendars you created remain until you remove them.

What is generally transmitted when content is loaded from the internet (IP address, time, app environment) is described in section 7.

4.1 Trip planning

Data
everything you enter: stops, places, dates, accommodation, legs, flights, booking references, notes, tasks, costs, attachments (PDF, images), archived trips.
Purpose
planning your trip and looking things up on the way.
Legal basis
You process this data on your device. It is not transmitted to me or to third parties. For storage on the device, see section 5.
Storage location
a database in the app's private storage, encrypted by default (section 9). The data only leaves the device when you trigger it yourself (4.4, 4.5).
Retention
until you remove entries, delete the trip in the archive or uninstall the app.

4.2 Settings

Data
language, appearance (light, dark, system), reminders and daily briefing with time, protection mode, travel alerts on or off, preferred navigation app, whether you granted location access, home screen layout.
Purpose
showing the app the way you set it up.
Legal basis
no transmission; storage under Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (section 5).
Storage location
on the device only. None of it is sent, not even the language.
Retention
until you change the setting or uninstall the app.

4.3 Importing a booking

Data
a booking confirmation (PDF or text) that you share with or paste into TravelFlow. Only name, dates, times, booking reference and amount are taken over – no names of fellow travellers, PINs or bank details.
Purpose
not having to type in bookings.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (a feature you requested).
Storage location
The file is read on the device and not uploaded. The extracted details are stored with the trip (4.1).
Retention
as in 4.1.

4.4 Backup, calendar file, budget image and sharing

Data
a backup (.tflow) of all trips, a calendar file (ICS) or an image of your budget.
Purpose
backing up data, moving it to another device or passing it on.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; you trigger each action yourself.
Storage location
wherever you save the file, or with the app you pick in the share menu. The backup is encrypted and can only be opened with the backup code shown. The recipient's own privacy terms apply.
Retention
until you remove the file.

4.5 Trip in the device calendar

Data
trip events: name, place, date and time, plus details such as nights, check-in times and flight number. The app deliberately does not add costs or booking references, not even to the calendar file.
Purpose
On request, the app creates a separate calendar "TravelFlow · trip name" per trip, updates it and removes it again.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Storage location
a local calendar on the device, without an account. It is not synced or uploaded. The events are stored outside TravelFlow's encryption. Other apps with calendar access can read them.
Retention
until you remove the calendar in TravelFlow or in the calendar settings.

4.6 Reminders and daily briefing

Data
a short text per notification, e.g. "Kyoto · Boat tour". It contains no booking references, notes or amounts.
Purpose
telling you about the day each morning and reminding you of appointments.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Storage location
The text is handed to Android in advance and stored there unencrypted until it is shown. It may be visible on the lock screen.
Retention
until it is shown or until you turn the reminder off.

4.7 Map

Data
the map area you are viewing (tile coordinates) and your IP address. This shows which area you are currently looking at.
Purpose
displaying the map.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: a working map without running my own map server.
Recipient
CARTO (section 7.1).
Storage on the device
The last map area and the last selected point are kept in memory only while the app is running. They are not stored on the device.

4.8 My location

Data
your device's position.
Purpose
showing your position as a dot on the map and centring the map on it.
How it works
The app only asks when you tap "My location" on the map. A sentence explains why first. Only then does Android's prompt appear.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Storage location
The position is only displayed, not stored and not sent. The app only remembers whether you granted access. If you centre the map on yourself, it loads the map tiles for that area from CARTO (4.7), which roughly reveals where you are.

4.9 Place search

Data
your search term, a fixed language setting (German and English) and a rough search area around the current stop; if you tap the map, the tapped coordinates. The search only runs when you trigger it (enter key or "Search"), not while you type.
Purpose
finding places and putting them on the map.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Recipient
my relay service (section 8), which only passes the search term or coordinates on to the OpenStreetMap Foundation – without your IP address (section 7.2).
Storage and retention
The app does not store search terms or results. Only when you pick a place is it stored with the trip (4.1). At the relay service: section 8.

4.10 Flight data

Data
flight number and date.
Purpose
adding flight times, terminal and status to the trip.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Recipient
my relay service (section 8), which queries a flight data provider – without your IP address (section 7.3). A flight number and date do not allow the provider to identify you.
Storage and retention
The response is stored with the trip (4.1). At the relay service: section 8.

4.11 Place photos

Data
name or Wikidata ID of the place, file name of the image, your IP address.
Purpose
preview and background images of places from freely licensed sources. Author and licence are shown with the image.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: a vivid place preview without running my own image server.
Recipient
Wikimedia Foundation (section 7.4), directly from the app.
Storage on the device
Previews and photos are cached while the app is unlocked and deleted when it locks (section 9).

4.12 Exchange rates

Data
no trip or budget data, only the request itself and your IP address.
Purpose
converting budget amounts into other currencies.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: current rates without running my own server.
Recipient
Frankfurter (section 7.5). The European Central Bank's reference rates are loaded at most every 12 hours.
Storage on the device
the most recently loaded rates, until new ones arrive.

4.13 Travel alerts

Data
no trip data, only the request itself and your IP address.
Purpose
showing travel and safety advice and natural hazards at your places.
How it works
At most every six hours, the app loads the complete lists from GDACS and – depending on the app language – from the German Federal Foreign Office (German) or the U.S. Department of State (English). It works out which alerts concern your trip on the device only. Travel alerts stay on until you turn them off in the settings. Tapping an alert opens the source's page in the browser.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: advice about your own trip from official sources.
Recipients
German Federal Foreign Office (7.6) or U.S. Department of State (7.7), GDACS (7.8).
Storage on the device
the most recently loaded lists, until new ones arrive. If you turn travel alerts off, they are deleted.

4.14 Navigation and opening a place in a maps app

Data
destination (coordinates, possibly name).
Purpose
If you tap "Start navigation" or have a place looked up in the maps app, Google Maps or Apple Maps opens with that destination.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Recipient
the provider of the chosen maps app (7.10). From then on, their privacy policy applies.

4.15 Screenshot protection

You can optionally block screenshots and screen recordings of the app. The setting is stored on the device only; no data is transmitted.

4.16 Contact by email

Data
your email address and the content of your message.
Purpose
answering your enquiry.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, otherwise (f) (legitimate interest: answering enquiries).
Recipient
My mailbox is hosted by Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland) as processor (7.12).
Retention
I delete the message once the enquiry is settled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

4.17 Age limit

TravelFlow is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Storage of and access to information on the device (Section 25 TDDDG)

TravelFlow only stores on your device what is needed for the features you use:

  • Trip data (4.1), encrypted by default.
  • Settings (4.2), including the language and whether you granted location access. The location itself is not stored.
  • Caches, so the app works offline and contacts servers less often: place previews and photos (4.11), exchange rates (4.12), travel alert lists (4.13).

Information on your device is only accessed via the permissions in section 6, and only when you use the feature. From the calendar, the app only reads its own trip calendars.

All of this is strictly necessary for the app to provide the features you request (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). No consent is required for it. There are no identifiers for recognising you and no advertising ID.

Recipients and third-party services

Every time content is loaded from the internet, the server necessarily receives your IP address, date and time and details of the app environment (e.g. the Android WebView user agent). Beyond that, only what is listed below is transmitted. All connections are encrypted via HTTPS.

TravelFlow contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting services and does not create usage profiles. The font ships with the app; no fonts are loaded from third parties.

7.1 Map (CARTO, OpenStreetMap)

The map is built from map tiles by CARTO (CartoDB Inc., 307 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10016, USA), based on OpenStreetMap data. When you view and move the map, the app loads the tiles for the visible area directly from CARTO. This shows which area you are looking at – and if you centre the map on your position, roughly where you are.

CARTO processes these requests on my behalf under its data processing agreement, truncates the IP address and, by its own account, keeps access logs for 30 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.7). Privacy notice: carto.com

7.2 Place search (OpenStreetMap Nominatim)

My relay service (section 8) only passes the search term or coordinates on to the Nominatim search service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom) – without your IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (4.9). Privacy notice: osmfoundation.org

7.3 Flight data (aviationstack, AeroDataBox)

My relay service (section 8) queries flight times, terminal and status from a flight data provider – without your IP address. Only flight number and date are transmitted, i.e. no data that can be linked to you. Providers are aviationstack (apilayer Data Products GmbH, Vienna, Austria) and, as a fallback, AeroDataBox (British Columbia, Canada), reached via the RapidAPI marketplace. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (4.10).

7.4 Place photos (Wikimedia)

For preview and background images, the app asks Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia and Wikidata directly for a suitable, freely licensed image and loads it from there. The operator is the Wikimedia Foundation, Inc., 1 Montgomery Street, Suite 1600, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA. The name or ID of the place and your IP address are transmitted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.11). Privacy notice: foundation.wikimedia.org

7.5 Exchange rates (Frankfurter)

The app loads the European Central Bank's reference rates via the Frankfurter service (Line of Flight B.V., Amsterdam, Netherlands). The request contains no trip or budget data. According to the operator, IP addresses are not logged. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.12).

7.6 Travel alerts: German Federal Foreign Office

If the app is set to German, it loads the complete list of travel and safety advice from the open data service of the German Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt, Werderscher Markt 1, 10117 Berlin, Germany). The request contains no trip data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.13). Privacy notice: auswaertiges-amt.de

7.7 Travel alerts: U.S. Department of State

If the app is set to English, it loads the complete list of travel advisories of the U.S. Department of State (travel.state.gov, Washington D.C., USA) instead of the Federal Foreign Office's list. The request contains no trip data; only the IP address is transmitted (7.13). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.13). Privacy notice: state.gov

7.8 Travel alerts: GDACS

The app loads the current event list of the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS), a joint service of the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (Ispra, Italy) and the United Nations. The request contains no trip data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (4.13). Terms of use: gdacs.org

7.9 Netlify

My relay services run at Netlify, Inc. (101 2nd Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA) on my behalf, under Netlify's data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. What arrives there and how long it stays is described in section 8.

7.10 Google Maps and Apple Maps

Only when you tap "Start navigation" or have a place looked up in the maps app does Google Maps or Apple Maps open with the destination. From then on, that provider's privacy policy applies.

7.11 Google Play

JueK3y servers

I do not run a server that receives your trip data. There are two small relay services on juek3y.com, hosted by Netlify (7.9):

  • Place search (4.9): receives the search term or tapped coordinates, language setting, search area and your IP address. Only the search is passed on to Nominatim.
  • Flight data (4.10): receives flight number, date and your IP address. Only flight number and date are passed on to the flight data provider.

How long it stays:

  • The services use your IP address only to limit excessive requests (place search at most 40, flight data at most 12 requests per minute). It is only held in the memory of the respective function instance and discarded when the instance ends, usually after a few minutes.
  • Place search results are cached without any link to you, so the same search does not reach Nominatim again: up to 7 days, at most one more day while refreshing.
  • Flight data is only kept, without any link to you, in the memory of the function instance.

What is logged: The services themselves do not write IP addresses, search terms or flight numbers to logs, only error messages without personal data. Netlify keeps technical function logs for at most 7 days.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the request (4.9, 4.10); for rate limiting via the IP address, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interest: protecting the services from abuse.

Visiting this page: When you visit this page, Netlify processes technically necessary access data (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) to deliver the page and keep it secure. This page sets no cookies and loads no third-party content.

Security

Encryption
Your trip data is stored in a database in the app's private storage. In the default mode it is encrypted with AES-GCM (256-bit); the key is held in Android's protected key store (Keystore). You can switch to unencrypted storage in the settings.
Unlocking
In the default mode, Android only releases the key once you confirm with your fingerprint, face or device screen lock.
Backups
The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You create backups yourself (4.4); they are encrypted with their own backup code.
Transmission
HTTPS only.
Screenshot protection
optional (4.15).
What is not encrypted
Caches for place previews and photos are stored unencrypted on the device while the app is unlocked. They are deleted when it locks. The app keeps the map area in memory only. Outside TravelFlow, the trip calendar (4.5) and the text of notifications (4.6) are also stored.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

As I do not store any data about you, I can generally not provide information about stored data. You manage your trip data yourself in the app:

change and remove
directly in the trip. You delete a whole trip permanently in the archive.
take it with you
as a backup or calendar file (4.4).
object
turn travel alerts off in the settings. The app only loads the map and place photos when you view them.

This app does not base any processing on consent. You can revoke permissions (section 6) at any time in the Android settings.

If you wrote to me (4.16), you can exercise your rights regarding that message with me: hello@juek3y.com
